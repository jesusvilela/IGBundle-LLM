01The Thesis

Language models learn representations in high-dimensional vector spaces, but these spaces are treated as flat, featureless, and interchangeable. IGBundle hypothesises that equipping the latent space with genuine differential geometry — curvature, parallel transport, symplectic structure — creates inductive biases that improve reasoning and faithfulness.

The adapter lives at a single transformer layer (layer 12 of Qwen 2.5-7B) and decomposes hidden states into base coordinates on a Poincaré ball (hyperbolic geometry), fiber sections (categorical distributions over K=16 components), and a Riemannian metric that couples them. Updates follow geodesics via the exponential map; energy is conserved via symplectic integration.

02The Critical Reckoning

In March 2026, an independent audit revealed that several key metrics were circular or hardcoded. The training script at train_odyssey_v3.py:1168 contained:

constraint_score: 1.0     # HARDCODED — never computed The "100% manifold faithfulness" was an assertion, not a measurement
The question was never “does the geometry look beautiful?” It was “does the geometry survive contact with evaluation, and does it produce measurably different behaviour from vanilla parameter-matched baselines?”

Rather than defend the claim, we designed a falsification experiment with a null hypothesis stated before seeing any results:

H0: Multi-layer vanilla LoRA ≥ single-layer geometric LoRA on task benchmarks.
If we cannot reject H0 at p < 0.05, geometry has no detectable signal at this scale. Null hypothesis — stated 2026-03-25, before any evaluation

05Interpreting the Geometry

Negative Curvature (K ≈ −5.63)

The Poincaré ball has constant sectional curvature −1, but the conformal factor 1 + 0.5 · tanh(||x||) modulates effective curvature. K≈−5.6 indicates the learned representations cluster near the ball boundary where distances grow exponentially — consistent with hierarchical structure in language.

Unfrozen Entropy (S = 0.952)

At initialisation, fiber sections are uniform: S = ln(16) ≈ 2.773 (maximum entropy). After 3000 training steps, S dropped to 0.952, meaning the 16 fiber components have specialised. The model has broken symmetry — different inputs activate different geometric pathways. JSD = 0.424 from uniform confirms this is not a collapsed mode but genuine diversity.

Symplectic Energy Conservation

The GENERIC (General Equation for Non-Equilibrium Reversible-Irreversible Coupling) framework demands that the reversible part of dynamics conserve energy exactly. Our symplectic integrator achieves drift < 0.0004% per step — near machine precision. This is not just a nice number; it means the Hamiltonian flow is structurally preserved, and the fiber bundle dynamics are genuinely symplectic rather than ad-hoc.
