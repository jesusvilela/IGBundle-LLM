Evidence, scope, and limitations

Updated 30 October 2026 · IGBundle is experimental research, not a validated model release.

Bottom line: The fiber-structured geometric-adapter idea remains a hypothesis worth testing. Available results do not demonstrate a general task-performance advantage or learned hyperbolicity. Older numerical claims were withdrawn; see the correction notice.

1. October 2026 local multi-benchmark evaluation

A local run on 27 October compared base Qwen2.5-7B, an exported merged cp300 checkpoint, and a reconstructed adapter composite. Evaluation used greedy decoding on fixed subsets ranging from 8 to 98 examples. The custom weighted index is descriptive, not a standardized benchmark and not a statistical test.

None of the per-task paired comparisons was significant. For the reconstructed composite vs cp300, McNemar p-values were 0.63–1.00. The composite was assembled post hoc and was not the original trained checkpoint; different checkpoint assembly and quantization/loading paths prevent isolating a geometric effect. Higher point estimates on some small subsets are not confirmation of benefit.

2. September 2026 paired token-NLL probe

On 24 held-out examples with 64 tokens scored per example, mean token NLL was 2.3188 for the frozen base, 1.9352 for geometric adapter A, and 0.8187 for vanilla-LoRA control B. B had lower NLL on all 24 pairs. Mean paired difference A − B was 1.1165 (95% paired t interval 0.9144–1.3186; p = 5.83 × 10−11).

This probe is not task accuracy and does not isolate geometry: A had 4,160,713 trainable parameters and one geometric layer; B had 585,728 parameters and 13 LoRA layers. The published file omits prompt text.

Download the 24 prompt-free paired measurements.

3. Operator diagnostics are not task evidence

A local audit reports 6/6 checks passed for the tested fixed operators/configuration. It can support only those implementation-level checks. It does not show that training learned geometry or that geometry improves language tasks.

The historical curvature readout around −5.63 comes from a fixed conformal component and is weight-invariant. It is not a learned curvature estimate. Accordingly, the phrase “learned curvature K = −5.63” is withdrawn.

5. What a stronger test needs

A causal evaluation should match parameter budget and layer placement; compare against a vanilla adapter under the same data, objective, optimizer budget, decoding, and hardware; isolate the geometric operator; use multiple seeds and fixed task splits; and publish exact checkpoint provenance with per-task outputs. Operator diagnostics should remain distinct from downstream outcomes.

No efficiency claim is made. The repository does not currently provide a matched, reproducible training-time, inference-latency, or memory comparison supporting earlier percentages.
