# Topic: Fiber Bundle Geometry in Neural Networks

## Research Questions
1. What prior work exists on applying fiber bundle theory to deep learning architectures?
2. How do connections and parallel transport relate to skip connections and residual streams?
3. What is the relationship between holonomy of a learned connection and model generalization?
4. Has anyone implemented genuine (not decorative) gauge equivariance in transformers?
5. What are the computational costs of maintaining geometric structure during training?

## Keywords
fiber bundle, principal bundle, gauge equivariance, connection, parallel transport,
holonomy, Ehresmann connection, associated bundle, structure group, neural network geometry
