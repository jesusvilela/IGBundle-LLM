# Topic: Geometric Steering and Controllable Generation

## Research Questions
1. What inference-time control mechanisms exist for LLM generation beyond temperature/top-p?
2. How does GENERIC (General Equation for Non-Equilibrium Reversible-Irreversible Coupling) apply to neural generation?
3. Can curvature and entropy be used as real-time feedback signals for generation quality?
4. What is the relationship between activation steering and geometric steering?
5. How do homeostatic controllers compare to classifier-free guidance for controllability?

## Keywords
controllable generation, activation steering, representation engineering, GENERIC framework,
homeostatic control, curvature feedback, entropy regulation, inference-time intervention,
Lyapunov stability, dynamical systems, generation quality
