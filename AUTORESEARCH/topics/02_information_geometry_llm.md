# Topic: Information Geometry of Language Models

## Research Questions
1. How does the Fisher information matrix of a transformer's output distribution relate to model uncertainty?
2. Can sectional curvature of the statistical manifold predict reasoning difficulty?
3. What is the natural gradient's effect on adapter fine-tuning convergence?
4. How does KL divergence geometry change across transformer layers?
5. What is the relationship between information-geometric curvature and loss landscape topology?

## Keywords
Fisher information, natural gradient, statistical manifold, KL divergence, Riemannian metric,
sectional curvature, information geometry, Amari, alpha-connections, dual connections,
exponential family, mixture family, transformer geometry
