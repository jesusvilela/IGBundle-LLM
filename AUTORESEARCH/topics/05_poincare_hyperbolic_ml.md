# Topic: Poincare Embeddings and Hyperbolic Machine Learning

## Research Questions
1. What are the advantages of Poincare ball embeddings over Euclidean for hierarchical data?
2. How does hyperbolic geometry interact with attention mechanisms in transformers?
3. What optimization challenges arise from Riemannian gradient descent on the Poincare ball?
4. Can hyperbolic representations improve reasoning in language models?
5. What is the current state of hyperbolic transformers and their benchmarks?

## Keywords
Poincare ball, hyperbolic geometry, Lorentz model, hyperboloid, Riemannian optimization,
exponential map, logarithmic map, geodesic, hyperbolic attention, tree-likeness,
Gromov hyperbolicity, Mobius addition, gyrovector space
